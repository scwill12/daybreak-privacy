Daybreak — Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Daybreak collects nothing about you. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts, and no third-party code of any kind in the app.

Where your writing lives

Your favorites, ratings, practice history, collections, your own affirmations, and your settings are stored on your device. If you have iCloud turned on, they sync through your own private iCloud account so your devices agree. That data belongs to your Apple Account. It is not sent to us, and we have no way to read it.

Purchases

Daybreak Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a purchase is active. We never see your payment details.

Health

If you allow it, Daybreak writes a State of Mind entry to the Health app when you log how a practice felt. Nothing else is written, nothing is read back for any other purpose, and the entries stay in Health under your control. You can turn this off at any time, and the rest of the app works the same without it.

Microphone and camera

Recording an affirmation in your own voice saves the audio on your device. Mirror practice shows the camera on screen while you speak and records nothing at all. Neither is uploaded anywhere, because the app has no way to upload anything.

Writing with AI

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Daybreak can write affirmations from a situation you describe. That runs on the device using Apple's on-device model. What you type is not sent to a server, to Apple, or to us.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by your device, from affirmations already in the app. Nothing is pushed from a server.

Children

Daybreak does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version ships with the app update that changes it, so what you are reading always matches the version you have installed.

Questions

The support link on Daybreak's App Store page reaches us.

The same text is in the app, under Settings → Privacy policy.
