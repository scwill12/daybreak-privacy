Daybreak — Support

Daybreak shows one affirmation at a time, full screen. Most of how it works is explained inside the app, under Settings → How Daybreak works.

Common questions

My widget shows something different from the app. Press and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget, and set “Show” to “What you’re reading”. That is the default, and it follows the app rather than rotating on its own.

I bought Pro and it still looks locked. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. If you bought on another device, make sure both are signed into the same Apple Account.

Does mirror practice record me? No. The camera is shown on screen and nothing is written to disk — there is no recording feature in mirror practice at all.

Where is my data? On your device, and in your own iCloud if you have it switched on. See the privacy policy. You can export everything, or delete it, from Settings.
